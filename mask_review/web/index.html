By name, all frames

Look at the labels nearest the cut. If those under it are animals, lower it; if those over it are not, raise it. ✓ keeps a label whatever the cut; ✕ removes it. Click a tile to see it in its frame.

Check the labels

  • Go through a frame: press S. Each animal comes up in turn; Enter if it is right, a number key or a typed name if not, Delete if it isn’t an animal.
  • Remove a wrong label: point at it and click its ✕ (or press X).
  • Go through one kind: press T for tiles of every animal with the same name. Click one to see it in its frame; Esc brings you back to the same place.
  • Choose the cut: press C for the scores of all labels and the ones nearest the cut; J and K move it.
  • Star the names you use most (☆). They get the number keys 1 to 9.
  • Fix a box by dragging its corners. B adds a missing animal.
  • Everything saves by itself, and others see it at once.
